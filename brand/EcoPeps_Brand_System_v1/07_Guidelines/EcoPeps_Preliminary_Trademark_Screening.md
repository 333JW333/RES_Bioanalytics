# EcoPeps preliminary name-screening memo

Date: 23 September 2026

This is a preliminary design-stage knock-out review, not a legal opinion or trademark clearance.

## Findings

1. EcoPeps is actively used by the peptide and reagent website at ecopeps.com.
2. An exact-case variant, “EcoPEPS,” is already used by an INRAE / Université de Caen ecology research team in France. The field differs, but the exact verbal overlap makes broader jurisdiction and class review important.
3. A French business directory also lists ECOPEPS as a trade name for advertising, marketing, and packaging services (SIREN 884 798 729). That overlap is closer to brand-development services and raises the urgency of a professional French/EU review.
4. General indexed-web queries did not surface a definitive exact-match USPTO or EUIPO registration. Absence from ordinary web results does not establish availability.

## Next clearance pass

- Search exact and similar forms in the USPTO system: ECOPEPS, ECO PEPS, ECO-PEPS, ECOPEP, ECO PEPTIDE, and ECOPEPTIDES.
- Search the same forms in EUIPO eSearch plus and WIPO Global Brand Database.
- Review relevant goods/services, especially International Classes 1, 5, 35, and 42 with counsel.
- Run an image-similarity search for the torus device in the USPTO search system.
- Obtain a trademark attorney’s registrability and freedom-to-use opinion before packaging spend or filing.
