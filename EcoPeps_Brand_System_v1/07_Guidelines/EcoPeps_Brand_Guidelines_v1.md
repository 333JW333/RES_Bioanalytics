# EcoPeps Brand Guidelines v1

## Identity architecture

- Display signature: 28 loops, metallic finish, 360 px / 45 mm minimum.
- Flat production mark: 20 loops, two-color linework, 40 px / 10 mm minimum.
- Micro mark: 8 loops, reinforced strokes, 16 px minimum.
- Center opening diameter: 10% of the full torus diameter across every new vector asset.

## Lockups

- Display: hero, social, launch graphics.
- Compact: header, navigation, documents.
- Stacked: square fields and centered layouts.
- Icon: avatar, app tile, favicon.

## Clear space

Use at least one center-opening diameter on every side.

## Core colors

- Brand Navy #071A2C — RGB 7/26/44 — CMYK 84/41/0/83
- Secondary Navy #0F2A45
- Peptide Teal #14B8A6 — RGB 20/184/166 — CMYK 89/0/10/28
- Dark Teal #0D8F81
- Ice #F6F9FB — RGB 246/249/251 — CMYK 2/1/0/2
- Graphite #17222F
- Neutral Line #E2E8F0
- Amber #FBBF24 — accent only

## Wordmark

The teal capital P is the customized cue. Use supplied outlined artwork rather than typesetting the logo. Geist remains the supporting website family; Helvetica, Arial, or Nimbus Sans are compatible production fallbacks.

## Control

Avoid stretching, changing the 10% aperture, recoloring loops, adding shadows to the reverse mark, using the detailed mark below 40 px, rotating the lockup, or introducing amber inside the logo.

## Trademark note

An INRAE / Université de Caen ecology team uses the exact-case variant “EcoPEPS.” A French business directory also lists ECOPEPS for advertising, marketing, and packaging services. Complete a legal clearance search before filing or packaging investment. This review is not a legal opinion.
